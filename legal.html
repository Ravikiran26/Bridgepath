Privacy, terms & responsible use.

Straightforward information about how Mindow handles data, protects children, and uses AI responsibly.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Child Safety Responsible AI Data Practices
The policies below reflect Mindow's current design principles and are provided for informational purposes. Final legal language will be reviewed and confirmed with counsel and school partners prior to public launch.

Privacy Policy

Mindow is built around a school-connected, privacy-conscious environment. We collect only the information needed to provide the Mindow experience — account and role information, and the content a user intentionally creates within the platform (Scribbles, Shelves and related expression).

What we don't do

  • We do not run targeted advertising on Mindow.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.
  • We do not use a child's expressed content to build a public profile outside the platform.

Access & control

Access to student data is role-based and school-connected. Parents and guardians have visibility and controls where applicable, consistent with school policy and local regulation.

Terms of Service

By using Mindow, schools, mentors and learners agree to use the platform for its intended purpose — curiosity-driven self-expression and experiential learning within a school-connected environment.

Acceptable use

The Social Wall and shared spaces within Mindow are moderated and school-connected. Content should reflect genuine curiosity, creation and learning — not unrestricted social interaction.

Account roles

Mindow accounts are provisioned through a school or institution with defined roles (administrator, mentor, student) and corresponding permissions.

Child Safety

Mindow is designed around a school-connected and controlled environment. Safety is built into the experience from the beginning, not added later.

  • School-connected access — accounts are provisioned by verified institutions.
  • Role-based permissions across administrators, mentors and students.
  • A moderated community, not an open social network.
  • Parental and guardian controls where applicable.

Responsible AI

Mindow uses AI to help organize, connect and interpret information that a user intentionally expresses within the platform — identifying expressed themes, connecting ideas, generating learning pathways and supporting mentors.

AI should assist understanding, not replace human judgment.

Mindow does not use AI to reduce a child to an AI-generated personality label, or to claim insight into what is happening inside a child's mind beyond what they have intentionally expressed, created, chosen or shared.

Data Practices

Mindow follows privacy-conscious data practices by design:

  • Data is used to power the Mindow experience — not for targeted advertising.
  • Data access follows role-based permissions tied to a learner's school.

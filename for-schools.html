Experience Mindow For Schools
For Schools

Not another LMS. Not another student portfolio.

Mindow creates a new interface between learner, mentor, AI, expression and experience — where curiosity, self-expression and experiential learning become part of the everyday learning journey.

Talk to Mindow See a Demo
Teachers & Mentors

New ways to communicate intent. New visibility into how students think.

Mindow gives mentors new ways to communicate learning intent and observe how students express their thinking.

Mentor → Student

One intent can become multiple experiences

A mentor expresses one learning intent through multiple mediums inside a single Scribble — text, sketch, image, voice, activity.

Student → Mentor

Reasoning made visible, not just output

Students can express reasoning, questions, ideas and creations through different forms — giving mentors richer visibility into how they approach an idea.

Student Experience

Express ideas. Ask questions. Draw. Create. Explore. Experiment.

Students document experiences, reflect, and continue into new questions — a journey that doesn't end when an assignment is submitted.

Express & create

Ideas, questions, drawings, voice and media come together inside a Scribble.

Explore & experiment

Test a hypothesis, build a proof of concept, and turn curiosity into a real experiment.

Reflect & continue

Document the experience, reflect on what happened, and continue into new questions.

Experiential Learning

The experience remains connected to the learner's expression.

Mindow connects thought, creation and experience — curiosity, thought, idea, create, test, experience, reflect, new curiosity — so learning becomes a continuous journey rather than a series of disconnected activities.

Safety & Responsible AI

Built for children. Designed around safety.

Mindow is designed around a school-connected and controlled environment. Safety is built into the experience from the beginning — not added later.

School-connected access
Role-based permissions
Moderated community
Parental/guardian controls where applicable
Privacy-conscious data practices
No targeted advertising
Responsible AI principles

AI assists understanding. AI does not define the child.

NEP-Aligned Learning

A future vision of digital identity

We move into the future by shifting from how you look to how you think.

For years, our digital identity has largely been represented by how we look. Our photographs became our digital identity. But as AI changes how we interact with technology, perhaps identity can evolve too.

Mindow explores a future where digital identity can represent more than a photograph — our curiosity, ideas, creations, interests, experiences and the way our thinking evolves.

What is Mindow?

A new interface between
curiosity, expression and experience.

Mindow brings together self-expression, AI-supported understanding, experiential learning and mentor–student interaction in one environment. Instead of forcing every learner into the same mode of expression, Mindow allows different minds to communicate in different ways.

Curiosity

Every learning journey on Mindow begins with what a student is genuinely curious to discover — not a fixed curriculum entry point.

Expression

Thought becomes visible through text, drawing, voice, images and interactive Scribbles — whatever medium feels natural to the individual.

Experience

Expressed intent becomes an actionable, experiential learning journey — connected from first thought to real-world application.

AI, used responsibly

AI that helps understand expression — not define the child.

Mindow uses AI to help organize, connect and interpret information that a user intentionally expresses within the platform. AI can help identify expressed themes, connect ideas, generate learning pathways and support mentors.

AI should assist understanding, not replace human judgment.

What our AI does

Organise a Scribble
Connect related ideas
Suggest resources
Identify expressed interests
Help create and summarise

But not

Diagnose personality
Assign intelligence
Decide a child's potential
Built for children

Designed around safety.

Mindow is designed around a school-connected and controlled environment. Safety should be built into the experience from the beginning — not added later.

School-connected access
Role-based permissions
Moderated community
Parental/guardian controls where applicable
Privacy-conscious data practices
No targeted advertising
Responsible AI principles

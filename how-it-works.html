How It Works

Concept → Interactive demonstration → Product experience.

Rather than explaining Mindow in slides, here's how each core idea actually works — Scribble, Shelf, the Intent Interface, and the loop that connects curiosity to growth.

Scribble

How I think & create.

A Scribble is more than a drawing or a note. It is an expressive space where thoughts, questions, drawings, words, images, voice, references, ideas and creations can come together — letting a person express an idea in the way that feels natural to them.

Intent Interactive Scribble

One Scribble. An entire learning experience.

Mentor ↔ Student

Redefining the mentor–student interface.

Mindow creates a two-way flow between mentors and learners.

Mentor → Student

One intent, many mediums

A mentor can express one learning intent through multiple mediums inside a single Scribble. Mindow can help transform that intent into different experiences for different learners.

Student → Mentor

Richer visibility, not just output

A student's Scribble can give the mentor visibility into the student's expressed reasoning, questions, ideas, creations and areas of curiosity.

AI Understanding

AI that helps understand expression — not define the child.

Mindow uses AI to help organize, connect and interpret information that a user intentionally expresses within the platform. AI can help identify expressed themes, connect ideas, generate learning pathways and support mentors.

AI should assist understanding, not replace human judgment.

What our AI does

Organise a Scribble
Connect related ideas
Suggest resources
Identify expressed interests
Help create and summarise

But not

Diagnose personality
Assign intelligence
Decide a child's potential
